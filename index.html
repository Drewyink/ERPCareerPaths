Questions and answers

How long is the training?

About four weeks of live sessions. Class meets Monday through Friday, 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM Eastern.

How long does it take to be ready for a job?

On average, 4 to 8 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly you complete your projects. If you need more time, you can sit in on the next class.

Is this a class or a job?

Treat it like a job, not a class. The class is the build. Each session works like a day at a company that runs on Dynamics 365:

  • Show up on time, the way you would for a shift.
  • Assignments come in like work tickets and requests from the business, with deadlines.
  • You build real deliverables: configurations, reports, flows, agents and documents such as BRDs.
  • Homework is part of the job.
  • Graduation is based on completing your projects, not on attendance alone.
Do we have access to the software?

Yes. Echolink Solutions provides all class software, including Dynamics 365 Business Central, Power BI, Power Automate, Power Apps and Copilot Studio. Usernames and passwords are provided to all registered students. The sign-in links are in the Software and reading materials section above.

When are office hours?

Monday through Wednesday, 9:00 AM to 10:00 AM Eastern. Come with any questions about class work, projects, certifications or your job search.

Are the roles remote?

It depends on the company. Some ERP, Power BI and AI roles are fully remote, some are hybrid and some are on site. Each job posting lists its work location, so check before you apply.

Do I need ERP experience or a degree?

No. The program is built for career changers, help desk and IT support staff, veterans and military spouses, and entry-level job seekers. No ERP experience is required.

I come from healthcare. Is this a good fit?

Yes. Hospitals and health systems run ERPs for supply chain, purchasing, finance, payroll and HR, alongside their EHR. Your healthcare background is an advantage: you already know departments, par levels, cost centers and why compliance rules like HIPAA matter.

  • ERP Business Analyst or Support Analyst at a hospital or health system
  • Supply chain systems analyst for medical and surgical supplies
  • Power BI developer building supply, staffing and finance dashboards
  • Copilot Studio developer building help desk agents for clinical and support staff

Open the How they connect tab and choose Healthcare to see a hospital example of all four tools working together.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not for most roles. Business Central, Power BI, Power Automate, Power Apps and Copilot Studio are low-code tools. Coding matters only if you choose the technical roles, such as ERP Technical Consultant (X++ or AL) or Power Platform Developer.

What computer do I need?

A laptop or desktop with a current web browser and a reliable internet connection. Power BI Desktop runs on Windows 10 or later. If you use a Mac, you can still build and share reports in the Power BI service in your browser.

How do I submit assignments?

Send each assignment by direct message to your instructor in Microsoft Teams. Do not post assignments in the class channels.

Which track should I choose?

Everyone starts with ERP, because every other track uses ERP data. Then follow what you enjoy:

  • Solving business problems and talking to people: ERP Business Analyst or Functional Consultant.
  • Numbers and dashboards: Power BI.
  • Removing manual steps: Power Automate and Power Apps.
  • AI agents: Copilot Studio.

Open the Career paths tab to compare roles and pay on each track.

Do I need a certification?

No. You do not need a certification to take this training or to get a job. A certification is optional. It helps solidify what you have learned and makes you stand out to employers and recruiters.

Which certification should I take first?

Certifications are optional. If you want one, match it to your track: MB-800 for ERP (Business Central), PL-300 for Power BI, AB-410 or PL-400 for Power Automate and Power Apps, and AB-730 then AB-620 for AI Copilot. PL-900 is a good first exam if you want a broad introduction. Always check the retired exams page before you study.

No training program can guarantee a job. What you get is hands-on projects, interview preparation, recruiter contacts and open roles, and help with your applications once your instructor confirms you are ready.

How ERP, Power BI, Power Automate and AI Copilot connect

The ERP is the system of record. Every other tool reads from it, acts on it or answers questions about it. Click any box or any line to see what passes between them.

Where the EHR fits. In a hospital, the EHR (Epic or Oracle Health, formerly Cerner) holds patient care records. The ERP runs the business side: supplies, purchasing, finance, payroll and HR. The two connect through healthcare standards such as HL7 and FHIR, often through services like Azure Health Data Services. Patient records stay in the EHR; the examples below use only supply and business data.
